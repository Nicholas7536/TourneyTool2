Strikers Club — Compact Handoff

## Purpose

Strikers is a population-ladder tournament simulator and browser app for a
custom “steal a player” soccer format. The original PoC explored whether the
format creates eliminations, comebacks, multiple finalists, and manageable
tournament lengths. The primary benchmark is 24 players; also support 15, 18,
21, 27, and 30.

## Settled tournament rules

- Teams start with three players. Legal active rosters are 2–4; five players
  locks a team as a finalist. The game supports 2v2 through 5v5 only.
- A normal match transfers exactly one selected player from loser to winner:
  `3+3 → 4+2`, `4+4 → 5+3`, and `2+2 → 3+1`.
- A team at one player is eliminated; that player is no longer assigned to a
  team. Finalists leave the active ladder and cannot challenge or be stolen
  from.
- Matchmaking is normally same roster size. The app also supports nearest-size
  matching and strict matching with emergency fallback. Emergency matching is
  allowed only when no same-size pair exists and must use the nearest active
  levels; teams still field at least two players.
- The tournament ends when the configured maximum finalist count is reached.

### Golden goal

If the same two teams have reported two alternating wins while trading the
same player, their next match is marked golden goal. The match result records
the winner (“first to score”), and the winner may select exactly `loser size -
1` players to transfer. The final losing player is unassigned and the losing
team is eliminated. Golden-goal transfers are explicit player IDs in the UI
and are validated server-side for count, uniqueness, and membership.

## Current implementation

- Browser client: `artifacts/strikers-simulator/src/App.tsx` and `src/index.css`.
- Express/Mongo server: `artifacts/strikers-simulator/server/app.ts`.
- Python statistical simulator: `strikers_tournament_simulator.py`.
- Rules contract: `TOURNAMENT_RULES.md`.
- Public room responses omit player tokens and include viewer identity:
  player ID, team ID, role, and live lead status.
- Every player window has a current-window notifier showing player, team, role,
  phase, and team roster.
- The development harness (`/?harness=1`) opens isolated player sessions,
  displays live player names, teams, rosters, and lead/non-lead status, and
  refreshes room state every two seconds. Harness setup requires
  `ENABLE_TEST_HARNESS=true`.
- Team leadership is derived from the live team `leadPlayerId`; do not rely on
  the original harness session snapshot after replacements or transfers.
- Golden-goal report forms show losing-team checkboxes and require the exact
  transfer count; do not fall back to automatically choosing roster members.

## Important state invariants

- A player belongs to zero or one team and cannot also be a substitute.
- `team.rosterSize === team.playerIds.length`.
- Active teams have at least two players; finalists have five; eliminated teams
  cannot enter matches.
- No team can have two active matches, and reported matches cannot be reported
  again.
- Only a host or the appropriate participating team lead can perform protected
  actions. Validate this on the server, not only in the UI.

## Development and verification

From the repository root:

```powershell
corepack pnpm --filter @workspace/strikers-simulator run typecheck
corepack pnpm --filter @workspace/strikers-simulator run build
```

If Corepack cannot resolve its user profile in the restricted environment,
invoke `artifacts/strikers-simulator/node_modules/.bin/tsc.cmd` and
`vite.cmd` directly.

For manual multiplayer testing, start the app/server with the required Mongo
variables and `ENABLE_TEST_HARNESS=true`, open `/?harness=1`, create a fresh
room, and open all player sessions. Exercise replacements, lead changes,
repeated matches, golden-goal selection, elimination, and finalist locking.

## Open questions / next work

- Use simulation results to tune whether emergency matching produces good
  tournament pacing; treat the current policy as configurable.
- Add automated API tests for repeated/concurrent challenge, accept, and report
  requests, especially golden-goal selection and elimination invariants.
- Decide whether goalkeeper rotation needs individual player modeling or remains
  match metadata.
